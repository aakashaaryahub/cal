VoxLink Child Safety Standards

Effective 8 November 2026

VoxLink is a voice chat app for adults. These standards explain how we prevent, find and act on child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), on VoxLink. They apply to every call, chat, profile name and bio on the service, and they are part of our Terms of Service.

Zero tolerance

VoxLink has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. It is strictly prohibited to use VoxLink to:

  • create, share, request or promote child sexual abuse material of any kind;
  • groom a child, or contact a child for any sexual purpose;
  • sexualise minors in conversation, names or bios, including role-play or "jokes";
  • sextort, threaten or pressure a child, or ask a child for images, videos or personal details;
  • facilitate the trafficking or sexual exploitation of children.

Any account found doing this is banned permanently and reported to the authorities.

Adults only

  • VoxLink is only for people 18 and older. Every user must state their age when signing up, and the app does not accept an age below 18.
  • Users must confirm they are 18+ and agree to our Terms of Service before creating an account.
  • If we learn that a user is under 18, we remove the account.

How to report

  • In the app: tap Report during a call or on the screen after a call, and choose "Underage user" or "Inappropriate or sexual content". You can also block the person, and they are never matched with you again.
  • By email: write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the person's name in the app and what happened.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call the police on 112 (India) or your local emergency number first. In India you can also report to the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or call the Childline helpline on 1098.

What we do with reports

  • Reports about child safety are reviewed first, ahead of all other reports.
  • When a report is confirmed, we immediately ban the account. A banned phone number can't be used to sign up again.
  • We preserve the information we hold about the account (such as the phone number, profile and call and chat records) so that it can be given to law enforcement.
  • We report child sexual abuse and exploitation to the relevant authorities, including Indian law enforcement through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, and to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.
  • We cooperate with valid requests from law enforcement.

Prevention

  • VoxLink is voice-only: there is no video and no profile photos.
  • Phone numbers are never shown to other users, and the app reminds everyone not to share personal details.
  • Calls are live and are not recorded by VoxLink.

Compliance with the law

VoxLink complies with all child safety laws that apply to it, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and reports to regional and national authorities as these laws require.
