VoxLink Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

VoxLink is a voice chat app for adults (18+) that connects you with people for anonymous voice calls. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Using VoxLink also means agreeing to our Terms of Service.

What we collect

  • Phone number, to create your account and sign you in with a one-time code. It is never shown to other users.
  • Profile details you enter: name, age, gender, city, country, an optional bio, and who you prefer to talk to.
  • Activity: your call history (who, when, how long), the people you follow, votes, blocks, and reports you make or receive.
  • Messages you send to friends in the app.
  • Purchases: the plan you bought and Google Play's order ID. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Technical data: when you were last online, and server logs (such as IP address) kept for security and troubleshooting.

Microphone and calls

The app uses your microphone only during a call you start or join. Calls are live audio between the two people on the call. We do not record calls or store their audio.

How we use it

  • To run the service: sign-in, matching, calls, chats, friends, and Premium.
  • To keep people safe: reviewing reports, and stopping abuse, spam and fraud.
  • To understand usage in aggregate (for example, number of calls per day) and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run parts of VoxLink for us, and only what they need:

  • Daily (daily.co): carries the live audio of calls.
  • PowersText: sends your sign-in code by SMS.
  • Google Play: processes Premium payments.
  • Render (render.com): hosts our servers and database.

Other users see your name, age, gender, city and country, never your phone number. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, calls, chats, follows, votes, blocks and reports are deleted straight away. Records of purchases are kept, without your name or phone number, for accounting and legal reasons. If an account was banned for breaking our Terms, we keep its phone number so it can't be used to sign up again.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Or on the web, without the app: delete your account here.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time in the app.
  • Block or report anyone from a call or their profile.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for corrections, by email.

Children

VoxLink is only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn of such an account, we delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in codes are stored only as one-way hashes and expire after 5 minutes.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
